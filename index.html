Systems I Build

My work sits where XR, AI, and robotics converge — where real‑time performance, determinism, and cross‑domain integration all matter at once.

These systems support spatial computing, high speed data streaming and caching, and cloud‑scale infrastructure.

  • Real‑time XR and robotics simulation engines for telepresence, surgical training, and manipulation.
  • Perception pipelines combining segmentation, smoothing, tracking, and neural inference for spatial understanding.
  • Distributed cloud architecture with multi‑VM NoSQL nodes, Kubernetes services, and efficient state synchronization across edge and cloud.
  • High‑performance rendering & interaction layers using compute shaders, procedural mesh instancing, ECS‑based collider systems, and Burst‑optimized math pipelines.
  • Spatial computing interface design, including MusicShaper — an 85,000‑line XR environment grounded in geometric music theory and backed by multiple granted patents and NSF SBIR awards.
